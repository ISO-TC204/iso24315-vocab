# emergent regulation

<div class="draft4-watermark" aria-hidden="true"></div>

[METR regulation](METR regulation.md) created and implemented while previously distributed, pre-announced [rule and regulation sets](rule and regulation set.md) are still [fresh](freshness period.md)

<object type="image/svg+xml" data="../../diagrams/emergent regulation.dot.svg">
    <img alt="emergent regulation Diagram" src="../../diagrams/emergent regulation.dot.png" /> <!-- Fallback for non-SVG browsers -->
</object>

Clause: 3.2.7.1

EXAMPLE: Immediately after downloading the current pre-announced rule and regulation set, the [jurisdictional entity](jurisdictional entity.md) issues a new [METR regulation](METR regulation.md) that closes a lane on a road due to a vehicular collision.

Note 1 to entry: Derived from SP-66,SP-133,SP-54.

Note 2 to entry: Emergent rules typically require alternate distribution mechanisms to ensure that [METR users](METR user.md) are notified in time to allow for operational decisions.

Note 3 to entry: Emergent rules can include both [temporary](temporary rule.md) and [persistent](persistent rule.md) rules.

History note: 2026: Revised to align with the updated definitions for [rule](rule.md) and [regulation](regulation.md).
