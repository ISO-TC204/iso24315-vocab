# pre-announced regulation

<div class="draft4-watermark" aria-hidden="true"></div>

[METR regulation](METR regulation.md) distributed through the normal periodic update mechanism

    <img alt="pre-announced regulation Diagram" src="../../diagrams/pre-announced regulation.dot.png" /> <!-- Fallback for non-SVG browsers -->
</object>

Clause: 3.2.7.2

Note 1 to entry: Derived from SP-133.

Note 2 to entry: Pre-announced rules do not require low-latency communications because they are distributed well enough in advance so that all users with up-to-date [rule and regulation sets](rule and regulation set.md) will have a copy of the rule.

Note 3 to entry: Pre-announced rules typically have relatively long [freshness periods](freshness period.md) but can be impacted by [emergent rules](emergent rule.md).

History note: 2026: Revised to align with the updated definitions for [rule](rule.md) and [regulation](regulation.md).
